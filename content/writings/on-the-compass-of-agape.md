<h2 class="dv-title">Meditating on the Compass</h2>
<p class="dv-sub">Definitions of the Hub and the Cross, set against the spatial doctrine of the figure, with the method of its practice</p>
</div>
<h3 class="dv-h1">A NOTE ON THIS DOCUMENT</h3>
<p class="dv-p">This text is a companion to the figure, not a replacement for it. The Compass is a transcendental meditation tool: a way of seeing, notated so that the marks can call the seeing back — for its author, and for another who comes to use it. What follows are definitions wrung from that seeing. They are offered as the present state of a tradition that has only ever proceeded by revision.</p>
<p class="dv-p">For that reason the document keeps its own history legible. Where a term was distilled from an earlier, richer, or more mythic form, that earlier form is named in the definition as a <i>deep face</i> — present as horizon and as lineage, never as instruction. The newcomer meditates on the plain term; the longer practitioner sees what it was distilled from and understands that they hold a <i>process</i>, not a stone. A figure that hid its own revision would read as received revelation and would invite belief. A figure that shows its revision reads as method, and invites practice. Since the Compass exists to break vices and delusions — including the delusion that one has received a final truth — a form that presented itself as final would contradict its own function. The document is built to enact what it teaches.</p>
<p class="dv-p">By the same principle, the <i>shadows</i> of the virtues — the characteristic disorders into which love falls when a virtue is overrun — are left unnamed. To meditate on a vice is to look at the Compass and find, for oneself, which virtue is missing and which way to turn toward its repair. A named vice is information; an unnamed one is work, and the work is the practice.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-note"><i>A note on origin. The figure was first drafted in an altered, heightened state and was afterward recovered and reworked over years by ordinary, deliberate effort — names scrambled or replaced, mythic scaffolding set aside, structure kept only where it proved sound. That recovery is the reason the tool stands on its own and asks nothing of the reader’s beliefs. Some of the source material survives elsewhere, with its own context; none of it is needed here, and the Compass is meant to be used without it.</i></p>
<h3 class="dv-h1">THE SPATIAL DOCTRINE</h3>
<p class="dv-p dv-lede"><i>Everything below is defined against this frame. The figure on the page is a single still projection of a three-dimensional contemplative object; the doctrine is what that object is when turned.</i></p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">I. The Two Cuts and the Quadrants</h4>
<p class="dv-p">Two independent divisions quarter the field. The <b>circle</b> is a radial cut: what lies inside is the <i>knowable</i>, what lies outside is the <i>unknowable</i>. The <b>centre line</b> is a horizontal cut: above it is the <i>conscious</i>, below it the <i>subconscious</i>. Laid over the same ground, and cut by the diagonals through the anchors, is a second and independent quartering — the four suits, marking the four epistemic modes (Philosophy, Science, Theology, Alchemy). Each mode takes its material from an anchor-pair and its method from a virtue-pair.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">II. The Enacted and the Received</h4>
<p class="dv-p">The circle is the membrane of agency. What lies inside it can be <i>done</i>, or reached by doing, and is known precisely by being performed — one knows forgiveness by forgiving. What lies outside it cannot be enacted; it is a <i>condition that obtains</i>, a ground one stands within rather than a move one makes. Hence the vertical triplets (Evidence, Doubt, Way; Grace, Compassion, Forgiveness) sit inside, and the horizontal conditions (Acceptance and its column; Attunement and its column) sit outside. The vertical is the axis of <i>enactment</i>; the horizontal is the axis of <i>condition</i>. You enact what emanates and you receive what returns; the inside–outside membrane is also a one-way / two-way membrane.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">III. The Guarded Path and the Open Ground</h4>
<p class="dv-p">The vertical is the <b>guarded path of enactment</b>: stationed (graded steps toward each pole), gated by terminal disciplines (Logic above, Humility below), and sealed by threshold-acts (Temperance, Kenosis) that <i>are</i> the rim they sit on. The horizontal is the <b>open ground of condition</b>: immediate, held by hub-dispositions (Nuance, Prudence), unstationed and ungated, porous straight through to the unknowable. Three features of the horizontal — no stations, no gatekeeper, no sealing threshold — all fall out of one fact: a condition cannot be guarded. Agape sits where the guarded and the open cross.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">IV. The Crystal and the Light</h4>
<p class="dv-p">Imagine a light shone through a crystal ball. The <b>light source is the Void</b> — not darkness but unmanifest radiance, the lamp behind all things. The <b>crystal is the self</b>, the one medium the light must pass through to become an image at all. The <b>four virtues are the faces of the crystal</b>, the points where ray meets glass. The <b>four worlds are the glints of refraction</b> at those faces — not places but appearances, what the light <i>does</i> as each virtue bends it: Cosmos off Logic, Ethos off Humility, Mythos and Logos off the equatorial dispositions. <b>Agape is the image the light forms</b> in passing through; <b>the Kingdom is that image landed whole</b>, the completion usually broken by shadow.</p>
<p class="dv-p">Within the crystal is the <i>knowable</i>: the inverted reflection of the outer world, held against the inner wall — and that held reflection is the self. We know only our own refracted, inverted version of what is outside, because that alone is in contact with the glass. Beyond the crystal is the <i>unknowable</i>: the image cast outward and the shadows it throws, never in contact, falling where we cannot stand to look.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">V. The Paradox of Temperance</h4>
<p class="dv-p">To meditate is to step <i>outside</i> the self and behold the whole apparatus at once — the image within the ball, the light on the environment, the shadows on the wall, even the Kingdom usually lost in shadow. To bring the virtues to <b>temperance</b> is to stand in the light without blocking it, so the image lands clean. But this cannot be done by will, for whoever steps into the light casts a shadow and ends the meditation. The Compass therefore gives not the <i>state</i> of temperance but the <i>direction</i> toward it: temperance seen, never seized; the Kingdom shown, and withheld. A humility is built into the centre of the figure — the highest thing it shows is the one thing one is told cannot be taken.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">VI. The Fixed Axis and the Turning Surface</h4>
<p class="dv-p">The virtues are the surface of the sphere and do not rotate; the worlds are the light that bounces off them. The <b>poles are the two gate-virtues</b> — Logic at the conscious pole, Humility at the subconscious pole — and the sphere turns on them. The <b>equator is the open horizontal</b>: Mythos and Logos, the hub-virtues, the conditions, with Agape at its centre. The <i>conscious / subconscious</i> cut is the equatorial plane; the gate-virtues are its poles, perpendicular to it, each belonging wholly to one hemisphere.</p>
<p class="dv-p">The <i>vertical</i> is the absolute by which the practitioner orients: its direction is fixed. The <i>horizontal</i> is the variable in which the practitioner works: it has no intrinsic direction. Which way the light runs across the flanks is set by the <i>handedness of the spin</i>, and the spin is set by the vice under contemplation — it is the turn that corrects the present distortion. Up and down are given; left and right are earned, read freshly each time according to what is being healed. The cross holds you; the spin works you.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">VII. The Two Readings</h4>
<p class="dv-p">The figure has two faces, because it is at once an event and a picture.</p>
<p class="dv-p"><b>As light — upward from the Void.</b>  The causal reading: the thing as it occurs. The beam rises from the unmanifest source, passes through the crystal, forms the image, and lands as the Kingdom. Read this way the spine is a single ascending beam, and each station is a stage the light passes through. This is reality as <i>process</i> — what is happening, whence and whither — and it is glimpsed only from outside the self, in meditation.</p>
<p class="dv-p"><b>As image — outward from Agape.</b>  The phenomenal reading: the thing as it appears to the one within the crystal. From the centre the picture radiates both ways at once, and Agape is not a stage but the still centre the picture is composed around. This is reality as <i>appearance</i> — what is seen, from where the seer stands, which is always the centre of one&#x27;s own crystal. This is the practitioner&#x27;s native reading.</p>
<p class="dv-p">The two disagree about what Agape is, and both are right: from outside, watching the beam, Agape is an event in the light&#x27;s passage; from inside, being the self, Agape is the fixed centre everything turns around. The light-reading is Agape seen from outside the self; the image-reading is Agape seen from within it.</p>
<h3 class="dv-h1">THE HUB AND THE CROSS</h3>
<p class="dv-p dv-lede"><i>The centre and the four virtues. Every later term takes its measure from these. The two hub-virtues are dispositions love is held with; the two pole-virtues are disciplines love arrives at.</i></p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="agape">Agape</b>  —  the image the light forms in passing through the crystal of the self; one’s sense of self extended into what lies beyond it. Of itself it extends without limit and toward no end, and it has no virtue of its own; it will overrun any single virtue taken alone. It is brought to temperance — to a holdable shape — only by all four virtues at once, and in being shaped it becomes the picture by which the self meets the world. It is therefore not something one <i>has</i> but the form one <i>becomes</i> when being shines through one: love given shape by the four faces it must pass through, and by nothing less. It is fixed at the centre; everything else turns around it.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="nuance">Nuance</b>  —  the disposition of sensitivity to the particular — the faculty that refuses to collapse the specific into the general, the person into the type, the moment into the category. A hub-virtue, held immediately against Agape on the western face toward Mythos: love does not journey to nuance, it loves nuancedly or it does not love at all. As a face of the crystal, it is where the light bends toward meaning. It is register-neutral, a grip love is held with in both the conscious and the subconscious. Held, it lets love see what is actually before it.</p>
<p class="dv-p"><i><b>Opens onto.  </b></i>Acceptance, its outer condition on the western flank — deep face <i>Pistis</i>.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="prudence">Prudence</b>  —  the disposition of practical wisdom — the faculty that fits means to ends, counts cost, and gives love a measure in action. The eastern hub-virtue, held immediately against Agape on the face toward Logos; like Nuance it is an immediate grip rather than a destination, and one does not approach it through stations. As a face of the crystal, it is where the light bends toward order. Register-neutral, the second hand love grasps with. Held, it lets love act without ruin.</p>
<p class="dv-p"><i><b>Opens onto.  </b></i>Attunement, its outer condition on the eastern flank — deep face <i>Gnosis</i>.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="logic">Logic</b>  —  the terminal discipline of the conscious mind — the testing faculty that demands coherence and submits love’s seeing to evidence and doubt before it reaches the world. Unlike the hub-virtues, Logic is a <i>gate</i>, not a grip: it sits at the rim, the last station of the conscious ascent, abutting Cosmos. It is register-bound — it belongs wholly to the conscious hemisphere and is its pole. As a face of the crystal, it is the upper contact where light meets glass and throws the glint that is Cosmos. Reached, it keeps love from believing everything it loves.</p>
<p class="dv-p"><i><b>Perfected.  </b></i>Logic brought to fullness through the other three virtues becomes <i>Amplitude</i> — sight of the true nature of the real and the direction of time. Its depth runs not outward onto a flank but upward along the spine.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="humility">Humility</b>  —  the terminal discipline of the surrendered self — the faculty that empties love’s grip, consents to be small, and lets love enter conduct without seizing it. The lower gate, the rim-virtue at the foot of the subconscious descent, abutting Ethos, and the pole of the subconscious hemisphere, register-bound to it absolutely. As a face of the crystal, it is the contact where light throws the glint that is Ethos. Reached, it keeps love from remaking what it loves.</p>
<p class="dv-p"><i><b>Perfected.  </b></i>Humility brought to fullness through the other three virtues becomes <i>Chaos</i> — the childlike freedom on the far side of complete surrender. Its depth runs downward along the spine.</p>
<h3 class="dv-h1">THE DEEP FACES OF THE CROSS</h3>
<p class="dv-p dv-lede"><i>The four virtues deepen in two directions. The hub-virtues deepen outward onto the equator, into returning conditions whose perfected forms are named. The pole-virtues deepen along the spine, into perfected forms that are their own apparent opposites, reached only by passing all the way through the discipline.</i></p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">The Returning Conditions</h4>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="acceptance">Acceptance</b>  —  the outer condition on the western flank, the unbounded ground that Nuance opens onto — what is given back to love when it meets the world with sensitivity to the particular. Not an act but a state that obtains: the standing-open to what is, as it is, before it is sorted or refused. Where Nuance is the grip love is held with, Acceptance is the atmosphere that grip reaches into; one is enacted from within the crystal, the other received from beyond it. Held in its plain form, it is the receptivity that lets meaning return.</p>
<p class="dv-p"><i><b>Deep face — Pistis.  </b></i>Faith not as belief but as the trust that the particular, fully received, is trustworthy: the transcendent perfection of Nuance, the acceptance that has become fidelity. It is the far shore of the western flank — a name for what returns.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="attunement">Attunement</b>  —  the outer condition on the eastern flank, the unbounded ground that Prudence opens onto — what is given back to love when it meets the world with practical wisdom. Like Acceptance it is received, not done: the fitting of self to the order of things, the resonance between one’s measure and the measure of what is. Where Prudence is the hand love grasps with, Attunement is the order that hand comes into accord with. Held plainly, it is the responsiveness that lets right action return.</p>
<p class="dv-p"><i><b>Deep face — Gnosis.  </b></i>Not secret knowledge but the knowing that comes of being rightly tuned: the transcendent perfection of Prudence, the attunement that has become understanding. It is the far shore of the eastern flank.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">The Perfected Poles</h4>
<p class="dv-p dv-lede"><i>Each pole’s perfection is its own apparent opposite, reached by going through it completely. The discipline that most constrains, fully kept, opens onto the widest sight; the discipline that most empties, fully kept, opens onto the freest play. The axis turns into its opposite at its ends. These two stations carry both readings.</i></p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="amplitude">Amplitude</b>  —  a station high on the upper spine, deep in the cosmic cap — the perfected form of Logic, reached only by passing through the discipline, its threshold of Temperance, and the glint of Cosmos. It is reason brought to fullness through the other three virtues: no longer mere testing but <i>sight</i> — the capacity to know the true nature of the real and the direction of time, which itself follows the rise and fall of an amplitude. Its lineage runs back through the navigation of probability; its plain face is the height of clear sight.</p>
<p class="dv-p"><i><b>As image.  </b></i>Read outward from Agape: how far up love’s reasoning can open before completion — the perfection of a virtue.</p>
<p class="dv-p"><i><b>As light.  </b></i>Read upward from the Void: the late stage where the nearly-complete projection takes on its readable waveform, the beam resolving into the shape that will land as the Kingdom.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="chaos">Chaos</b>  —  a station deep on the lower spine, within the polar floor — the perfected form of Humility, reached only by passing through the discipline, its threshold of Kenosis, and the glint of Ethos. It is emptying brought to fullness through the other three virtues: no longer self-diminishment but <i>liberty</i> — the childlike freedom of a self that, having released every grip, can move any direction; the generative unruliness of the Monkey King. It is not disorder against the world but the open spontaneity on the far side of complete surrender.</p>
<p class="dv-p"><i><b>As image.  </b></i>Read outward from Agape: how far down love’s emptying can open before it reaches the source — the perfection of a virtue.</p>
<p class="dv-p"><i><b>As light.  </b></i>Read upward from the Void: the stage just above Being where the rising radiance first stirs into generative variety, the source’s first differentiation before it is disciplined into order higher up.</p>
<h3 class="dv-h1">THE FOUR ANCHORS</h3>
<p class="dv-p dv-lede"><i>Cosmos, Logos, Mythos, Ethos — the four worlds. They are not places but appearances: the glints of refraction thrown as love’s light bends at each face of the crystal. They take their formal meaning from Structural Absurdism, where they are the four aspects of human paradigm formation; their place on the figure from the virtue that bends the light; and their order from the cascade that binds them.</i></p>
<p class="dv-p">Read as light, the anchors form a <b>cascade</b>: <i>Cosmos → Logos → Mythos → Ethos</i>, each term limiting the next — the cosmos limits the order available to the logos, which limits the symbols available to the mythos, which limits the archetypes that shape the ethos. This is the genesis of a worldview: reality as process, descending from the world-as-it-is, through its order and our imagining of it, into the scaffolding of conduct. On the sphere it traces a descending helix — from the northern cap to the eastern equator, across to the western equator, down to the southern cap. The cross of the four held at once around Agape is the same set read as image: the faces love wears. The cascade is the beam; the cross is the picture.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="cosmos">Cosmos</b>  —  the real world as it is, as we are discovering it — the world we cannot hold whole, whose gaps the mind fills by abstraction to form a totality that satisfies its inner logic. On the figure, the glint thrown when love’s light bends through <b>Logic</b>: the world appearing as <i>ordered totality</i>, all-that-is grasped as coherent. Polar, at the conscious pole, and cast outside the circle of the knowable — the true cosmos always exceeds the self that would contain it; one only ever approximates it. The human bridge toward it is science and cosmology. First term of the cascade: that from which all else descends and which limits all the rest.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="logos">Logos</b>  —  the functional operation of the cosmos, the order and generation of all things; the order of the universe as it bears on the human condition — the meta-ethical factors that incentivise behaviour. A person’s logos grows more attuned to the cosmos as their cosmology clears. On the figure, the glint through <b>Prudence</b>: the world appearing as <i>navigable order, reason, word, and law</i>, grasped by practical wisdom. Equatorial, on the eastern rim; its outer condition is Attunement (deep face Gnosis) — a word the formal definition itself reaches for. Second term of the cascade: the cosmos’s operation, which limits the symbols available to the mythos.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="mythos">Mythos</b>  —  what we imagine reality to be in lieu of absolute truth — the collective symbols, associations, and archetypes a culture builds, the shared and blended paradigm we communicate. Since all human knowledge is approximate, all human paradigms are technically mythos: it is the medium we actually live in, not how the world truly is. On the figure, the glint through <b>Nuance</b>: the world appearing as <i>meaning and story</i>, the particular bearing weight, grasped by the sensitivity that refuses to let the blend collapse into a single myth. Equatorial, on the western rim — the ambient medium, porous, always being separated from the truer terms by the mental arts. Third term of the cascade: the imagined order through which conduct forms. Its disorder is the veil mistaken for the world (see Lineage).</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="ethos">Ethos</b>  —  the structure of human behaviour that forms within us through the mythos — the <i>inner Logos</i>, a self-orienting system that builds its own structure through observation, conditioning, and rationality: the scaffolding of the persona. On the figure, the glint through <b>Humility</b>: the world appearing as <i>character and conduct</i>, the lived field of acting-with-others, reached by the emptying that lets love enter conduct without seizing it. Polar, at the subconscious pole, cast outside the circle — it forms below deliberate knowing. Final term of the cascade: the inner order into which the whole descent settles. That it is the inner Logos binds the southern pole to the eastern equator — the world’s order, passed through the veil of imagination, becomes the order within a person.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">Lineage — Quelleity</h4>
<p class="dv-p">The anchors descend from Quelleity, the oldest prototype paradigm in this tradition — a theology grounded in understanding the nature of the world in order to motivate good action. Its positive triad ran <b>Quelos</b>, the primal fact that anything exists at all; <b>Tellos</b>, the generation of things into order; and <b>Gia</b>, the inherited inner spirit that connects us to all other things. To these was later added <b>Maya</b>, the warping veil of perception that deludes us. In maturing into the present anchors, Quelos became Cosmos, Tellos became Logos, Gia became Ethos — and Maya became Mythos.</p>
<p class="dv-p">The development is itself a teaching. Where the early paradigm held the veil apart as an external enemy, the mature one recognises that conduct forms <i>through</i> the veil — since all paradigms are approximate, there is no escaping mythos, only navigating it. So Maya was not defeated but reframed as the medium, and the work of navigating it was given to Nuance. The same move that sanitised the virtues runs here: an enemy reframed as a condition to be worked. Few fragments of Quelleity survive; it is recorded only to keep the tradition’s revision legible.</p>
<h3 class="dv-h1">THE VERTICAL SPINE</h3>
<p class="dv-p dv-lede"><i>The bounded path of enactment. It is one line read two ways — as light, the causal beam rising from the Void to the Kingdom; as image, radiating outward from Agape toward both ends. The upper arm is the conscious ascent through Logic to completion; the lower is the subconscious descent through Humility to ground. Both ends are true termini, because the figure is bounded: to pass beyond the Kingdom or beneath the Void is to leave the light. The two arms mirror, five stations each — inner triplet, threshold, motion, perfected pole, terminus.</i></p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">The Inner Ascent — the conscious stations</h4>
<p class="dv-p dv-lede"><i>Between Agape and the gate of Logic: the deliberate, epistemic steps by which love reasons its way upward. Read outward from Agape, Way is nearest the centre and Evidence nearest the gate.</i></p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="way">Way</b>  —  the innermost station, nearest Agape — the path love first sets out on, the initial orientation of the seeking mind before it is questioned or tested. Love’s first step upward into reasoning.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="doubt">Doubt</b>  —  the questioning the way provokes — not the enemy of reason but its living interior, the honest uncertainty without which reason hardens. The sublime logic hidden in doubt: a Logic that has expelled its doubt has become mere certitude.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="evidence">Evidence</b>  —  the station nearest the gate of Logic — the testing that doubt demands, the weighing of what is so, the culminating discipline before Logic seals the upper arm.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">The Inner Descent — the subconscious stations</h4>
<p class="dv-p dv-lede"><i>Between Agape and the gate of Humility: the felt, relational forms bounded love takes as it discharges downward into conduct. Read outward from Agape, Grace is nearest the centre and Forgiveness nearest the gate — the mirror of the ascent.</i></p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="grace">Grace</b>  —  the innermost station, nearest Agape — the first unearned gift, love extended before it is deserved or asked: the spontaneous overflow of bounded love downward.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="compassion">Compassion</b>  —  the feeling-with that grace opens into — love entering the condition of the other, suffering alongside rather than above.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="forgiveness">Forgiveness</b>  —  the station nearest the gate of Humility — the release of debt, the letting-go that culminates the descent and opens into the self-emptying of Humility.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">The Upper Cap — Temperance to the Kingdom</h4>
<p class="dv-p dv-lede"><i>Outward beyond the gate of Logic and its glint of Cosmos, the conscious arm completes itself toward fullness.</i></p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="temperance">Temperance</b>  —  the upper threshold-act, the rim seen from the conscious side — the disciplined edge that knows where knowing must stop. It bears the name of the state the whole cross produces: love brought to proportion by all four virtues at once. The crown of the figure is named for what the figure is for. With Kenosis it forms the joint seal — held together, the ascent and descent are consummated and the Heavens meet the Earth.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="adaptation">Adaptation</b>  —  the motion between the threshold and the perfected pole — the supple refitting by which a tempered mind keeps adjusting to what is, rather than freezing at its own limit.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="amplitude">Amplitude</b>  —  the perfected pole of the upper arm — Logic perfected, sight of the true nature of the real and the direction of time (defined in full under <i>The Deep Faces of the Cross</i>). Here it also marks a bound: the span within which one may move; to pass outside the amplitude is to fall back toward the Void.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="multiplicity">Multiplicity</b>  —  the motion between the perfected pole and completion — the distribution of a unity into many, so that no single part can dominate the whole. The structure by which a completed thing keeps from tyrannising itself; the anti-domination form just below the apex.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="kingdom">The Kingdom</b>  —  the upper terminus — completion, the image landed whole, set above the gate of Logic, far from yet near to the source. As light, the screen the beam finally strikes; as image, fullness, the picture entire. A true edge of the figure: not a wall but the limit past which one leaves it.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">The Lower Cap — Kenosis to the Void</h4>
<p class="dv-p dv-lede"><i>Outward beneath the gate of Humility and its glint of Ethos, the subconscious arm completes itself toward ground — the exact mirror of the upper cap.</i></p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="kenosis">Kenosis</b>  —  the lower threshold-act, the rim seen from beneath — the emptying that releases the grip on the knowable and lets it open into ground. Where Temperance holds the limit, Kenosis releases it; the two are one membrane seen from its conscious and its subconscious sides. With Temperance, the joint seal of the spine.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="evolution">Evolution</b>  —  the motion between the threshold and the perfected pole — the generative unfolding by which an emptied self develops rather than merely collapses. Mirror of Adaptation.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="chaos">Chaos</b>  —  the perfected pole of the lower arm — Humility perfected, the childlike freedom and generative unruliness on the far side of complete surrender (defined in full under <i>The Deep Faces of the Cross</i>).</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="being">Being</b>  —  the motion between the perfected pole and the ground — bare existence, the “is” still shadowed by its own negation, to be yet not be: the last station before the source. Mirror of Multiplicity.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="void">The Void</b>  —  the lower terminus — not darkness but unmanifest radiance, the nameless ground over which all is suspended and through which all emanates: oblivion as source. As light, the lamp behind all things; as image, the abyss-ground. The other true edge of the figure.</p>
<h3 class="dv-h1">THE HORIZONTAL CONDITIONS AND COLUMNS</h3>
<p class="dv-p dv-lede"><i>The open ground of condition. Where the spine is enacted, the flanks are received; where the spine is bounded, the flanks run on. These terms lie outside the circle — not acts but conditions that obtain, grounds one stands within. They are unstationed and ungated, and they circulate: returning inward to Agape as much as they emanate from it, in a direction set by the spin the vice calls for. Each term has a counterpart at the same altitude on the opposite flank — the western column of meaning answering the eastern column of order, across the equator.</i></p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">The Two Conditions</h4>
<p class="dv-p">At the centre of each flank, flanking Agape, sit the two conditions already defined under <i>The Deep Faces of the Cross</i>: <b>Acceptance</b> on the west (deep face Pistis), the receptivity that lets meaning return; and <b>Attunement</b> on the east (deep face Gnosis), the responsiveness that lets right action return. They are the near shores; their deep faces, Pistis and Gnosis, are the far shores where each flank terminates in the unknowable.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">The Western Flank — the column of meaning</h4>
<p class="dv-p dv-lede"><i>The Mythos side, guarded by Nuance. Above the centre lies the Philosophy quadrant (Cosmos × Mythos), below it the Theology quadrant (Ethos × Mythos). Read top to bottom, meaning descends from its reach toward totality down into conduct.</i></p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="abstraction">Abstraction</b>  —  the mind’s predictive modelling — understanding as the satisfaction of binding abstractions whose predictive value lets us interpret reality; the hole-filling by which a coherent Cosmos is formed. The highest western term, where meaning first reaches toward totality. Answers Relativity across the equator.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="philosophy">Philosophy</b>  —  the disposition that seeks to understand the world and communicate its processes (the mode itself is defined under <i>The Four Modes</i>). Answers Science across the equator.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="wonder">Wonder</b>  —  the one positive emotional default reachable without delusion — awe joined to critical analysis before a hard world; the affective root of philosophy. Answers Theory across the equator.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="harmony">Harmony</b>  —  sustained right proportion, requiring both power and grace in measure — the settled balance meaning comes into when its parts are held together. It is secured from the spine by Temperance, a higher virtue than grace or freedom for the keeping of harmony. Answers Existence across the equator.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="quintessence">Quintessence</b>  —  the reachable universal essence — the intrinsic nature of things beyond their physical form, dived for in imagination and surfaced as a pearl of understanding. It is bounded against Transcendence: theology has quintessence but not transcendence, and its discipline is to reach the universal essence without claiming the genuinely transcendent beyond. Answers Autopoiesis across the equator.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="faith">Faith</b>  —  pragmatic, formative belief — a chosen orientation written into the soul for its shaping effect, held honestly alongside not-knowing. Not certainty but the voluntary trust that forms a self; the affective root of theology. Answers Individuation across the equator.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="theology">Theology</b>  —  the art of meaning held before the mystery (the mode itself is defined under <i>The Four Modes</i>). Answers Alchemy across the equator.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="connection">Connection</b>  —  the bond of relation and belonging — our placement in the web of others, the tie by which meaning binds persons to one another and to the whole. The lowest western term, where meaning grounds into conduct; the meaning-side counterpart to Freedom’s liberty. Answers Freedom across the equator.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">The Eastern Flank — the column of order</h4>
<p class="dv-p dv-lede"><i>The Logos side, guarded by Prudence. Above the centre lies the Science quadrant (Cosmos × Logos), below it the Alchemy quadrant (Ethos × Logos). Read top to bottom, order descends from its reach toward the real down into liberated conduct.</i></p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="relativity">Relativity</b>  —  the frame-dependence of order — that any observation is defined relative to a reference and never grasped absolutely. The highest eastern term, and the objective relativity answering Abstraction’s subjective modelling; together they are the relative, abstracted nature of all knowledge. Answers Abstraction across the equator.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="science">Science</b>  —  the replicable logic of prediction applied in experiment (the mode itself is defined under <i>The Four Modes</i>). Answers Philosophy across the equator.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="theory">Theory</b>  —  the conscious recognition of natural pattern — the model-building born of an identity engaging the medium of its work, the structured cognition science runs on. The cognitive counterpart to Wonder’s awe. Answers Wonder across the equator.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="existence">Existence</b>  —  what is observed to have properties — space, time, and properties: the bedrock the rational order is read off of. Answers Harmony across the equator.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="autopoiesis">Autopoiesis</b>  —  self-creation — the gradual self-making by which a self conditions and produces itself, bit by bit; the engine of alchemical transformation. Answers Quintessence across the equator.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="individuation">Individuation</b>  —  the carving of one’s own nature through testing and relation, until one ceases testing and abides by one’s own nature — the becoming-oneself that alchemy works toward. Answers Faith across the equator.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="alchemy">Alchemy</b>  —  the shaping of the soul, guided toward reality with love of freedom (the mode itself is defined under <i>The Four Modes</i>). Answers Theology across the equator.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="freedom">Freedom</b>  —  the fundamental power to choose — the ground of trust between persons and of a collective’s capacity to organise; the lowest eastern term, where rational order grounds into liberated conduct. Answers Connection across the equator.</p>
<h3 class="dv-h1">THE FOUR MODES</h3>
<p class="dv-p dv-lede"><i>The four epistemic modes occupy the quadrants — the second, diagonal quartering of the field. Each is a composition of terms already defined: it takes its material from an anchor-pair and its method from the two virtues that bound its quadrant, draws its temper from a prime attribute on the columns, speaks in a prime form of expression, and keeps to a discipline stated as a boundary-rule — what lies within its quintessence, and the overreach just outside it that it must not become. Because each mode composes the anchors, virtues, and columns, this block is the test of whether the whole figure holds together.</i></p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="philosophy">Philosophy  ♠</b>  —  the mode of the north-west quadrant. Material: Cosmos × Mythos — the world-as-it-is met with meaning. Method: Logic × Nuance — reason joined to sensitivity for the particular. Its prime attribute is <i>Wonder</i>; its prime expression <i>That-Which</i>, the gesture that points at and names what a thing fundamentally is. Philosophy is the wonder-led interpretation of the world’s processes, structured by reason yet attentive to the particular. Its discipline: <b>absurd structure but not naturalism</b> — it keeps the definite structures found within an absurd process but refuses to reduce meaning to mere nature.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="science">Science  ♦</b>  —  the mode of the north-east quadrant. Material: Cosmos × Logos — the world-as-it-is met with order. Method: Logic × Prudence — reason joined to practical wisdom. Its prime attribute is <i>Theory</i>; its prime expression <i>Is-As</i>, the predicative modelling that says what a thing behaves or can be cast as. Science is the replicable logic of prediction applied in experiment, the model tested against the real. Its discipline: <b>relativity but not correlation</b> — it keeps the frame-dependence of observation but refuses to mistake correlation for law.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="theology">Theology  ♥</b>  —  the mode of the south-west quadrant. Material: Ethos × Mythos — conduct met with meaning. Method: Nuance × Humility — sensitivity joined to surrender. Its prime attribute is <i>Faith</i>; its prime expression <i>May-Be</i>, the subjunctive of possibility and hope that holds what may be without asserting it. Theology is the faith-led holding of meaning before the mystery, shaping conduct through what is humbly believed. Its discipline: <b>quintessence but not transcendence</b> — it dives for the reachable universal essence but refuses to claim the genuinely transcendent beyond.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-entry"><b class="dv-term" data-goto="alchemy">Alchemy  ♣</b>  —  the mode of the south-east quadrant. Material: Ethos × Logos — conduct met with order. Method: Prudence × Humility — practical wisdom joined to surrender. Its prime attribute is <i>Individuation</i>; its prime expression <i>Un-Like</i>, the transformative un-likening by which a self is made other than it was. Alchemy is the shaping of the soul, the self worked toward its own nature and guided toward reality with love of freedom. Its discipline: <b>autopoiesis but not induction</b> — it makes the self by gradual self-creation but refuses to derive it from general rule.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">The Intersections</h4>
<p class="dv-p">The modes are not sealed quadrants but a connected field, and named arts live where they meet. Where two modes are adjacent, sharing a virtue between them, an art sits on that border: <b>ontology</b> between Philosophy and Science (the reach toward what is), <b>pneumatology</b> between Philosophy and Theology (the reach toward spirit), <b>therapy</b> between Science and Alchemy (the reach toward repair), and <b>divination</b> between Theology and Alchemy (the reach toward what is hidden).</p>
<p class="dv-p">Two further arts do not lie on the written plane at all. The page holds only two of the figure’s axes — the spine and the flanks are its x and y — and these arts occupy the third: the z-axis, running perpendicular to the figure through Agape. They ask a full dimension more of it. In front of the compass rests the <b>Arcane</b>, the meeting of Science and Theology across the centre; behind it rests the <b>Sublime</b>, the meeting of Philosophy and Alchemy. Neither is written on the figure, and deliberately so — they are named only here. These are powers of reconstruction and deconstruction of the soul, and the tradition holds a firm guardrail around them: neither should be practised before Agape is understood, lest they pass outside its order.</p>
<p class="dv-p">And there is a seventh, which does sit on the dial — at the very centre, where all the arts are practised at once but turned outward: the charitable way of <b>Agape</b>, done not for oneself but for others. So the field of the modes resolves, as the whole figure does, to love at its centre.</p>
<h3 class="dv-h1">MEDITATING ON THE COMPASS</h3>
<p class="dv-p dv-lede"><i>The definitions above are the map; what follows is the walk — one full session, from the arrival of a vice to the sentence carried out of it. The stages are given in order for the learning of them, but the practice is a single continuous act of attention; the divisions are for teaching, not for performing.</i></p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">I. The Inward Course</h4>
<p class="dv-p">The meditation does not begin at the figure. It begins with the vice, and inward. The practitioner arrives carrying a felt disorder — the initial perception of the vice, its surface presentation — and introspection descends from that presentation toward cause: what is actually generating it. This course is run in full before the compass is consulted. The figure structures nothing here; this stage produces the raw material the figure will later structure.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">II. Placement</h4>
<p class="dv-p">With the cause surfaced, turn to the compass and place the vice against the two cuts. The centre line asks whether it lives in the conscious, rational mind or below, in the subconscious. The circle asks whether it is something enacted, inside the knowable, or a condition received, on the open ground beyond. The answers give the vice its coordinates, and with them the character of the work: a vice placed on the guarded vertical has stations and gates to walk; one placed on the open horizontal is atmosphere to be moved through, not a path to be climbed.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">III. Calibration — the Two Readings</h4>
<p class="dv-p">Before anything is corrected, test the two readings against your own grip. Hold the figure as <b>projection</b> — the light, the causal beam rising from the Void, the thing as temporal process. Then hold it as <b>emanation</b> — the image, radiating outward from Agape, the thing as it appears from the centre. Alternate between them and notice which comes easier. The easier reading reveals the mind’s present posture — projecting temporally, or seated in the immanent — and the reading is itself diagnostic: the one that cannot be held may be precisely the sight the vice has cost.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">IV. Setting the Spin</h4>
<p class="dv-p">Up and down are given; left and right are earned. The vice sets the handedness: the spin is the turn that would carry the placed vice into contact with the virtue it has been starved of. Setting it converts the figure from map to path — the coordinates cease to be positions and become an order of passage, a method of moving through them toward the needed virtue.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">V. The Walk of the Eye</h4>
<p class="dv-p">Fix on the placed position and imagine it emanating or projected, as the calibration has shown. Then, under the spin, let the eye wander the figure and notice where it rests. At each resting point apply the one test: does this merely <i>compel</i>, or does it <i>turn inside</i>? The compelling is considered and passed. The point that turns — that elicits the gut response — is the live wire, marking the true site of the work. Dwell in the interactions; move between points and let them qualify one another. A traverse taken too fast arrives at a solution manufactured too cheaply.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">VI. The Narrative</h4>
<p class="dv-p">Around each turning point, widen to the pattern: the state of the lines, the connections they imply, all of them imagined moving toward the solution of the vice — what it means to pass from one point to another. Out of that movement, construct the meaning narrative. It is not retrieved from the figure; it takes shape in the traverse, and it is the practitioner’s own — compelling, and honestly manufactured.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">VII. The Garden</h4>
<p class="dv-p">Every meditation closes at the centre. Ask: what is the shape of the solution around my love for all the things I love? Some vices are love overrunning — there the <b>garden</b> is a limit, and the question is how to build it. Others are love withheld — there the garden is an opening, and the question is how to expand. Either way the garden is cultivation, not mere restraint: the tempered, holdable form love takes once the disorder has been walked back to Agape.</p>
<h4 class="dv-h2">VIII. The Garden Mantra</h4>
<p class="dv-p">Last, compress the garden into a single sentence — the <b>garden mantra</b> — short enough to repeat, reminding of the structure of the virtue just built. It is the marks calling the seeing back at the smallest scale: one line, spoken when the vice next arrives, that reconstitutes the garden without re-walking the figure.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-note"><i>A session, then: the inward course to cause; placement against the cuts; calibration by the two readings; the spin; the walk of the eye; the narrative; the garden; the mantra. The figure is the shared method; the garden is the particular solution; the mantra is the key to it.</i></p>
<h3 class="dv-h1">THE CENTRE HOLDS</h3>
<p class="dv-p">This completes a first full pass of the figure, defined from the centre outward: Agape and the four virtues; their deep faces and perfected poles; the four anchors and the cascade that binds them; the vertical spine of enactment, bounded at the Void and the Kingdom; the horizontal flanks of condition, open and circulating; and the four modes, where anchors, virtues, and columns compose and the figure proves it holds together; and the method of the meditation, by which the figure is walked.</p>
<p class="dv-p">Everything has resolved to the same point it began from. The modes meet at Agape; the spine is Agape seen as height and depth; the flanks are Agape received and returned; the virtues are how Agape is held to a shape it would otherwise overrun. The figure is, in the end, a single claim drawn four ways and turned in three dimensions: that love, of itself limitless, becomes a self by being given measure, and finds the Kingdom it cannot seize by walking, patiently, around the dial.</p>
<p class="dv-p dv-note"><i>These definitions, and the method of their use, are the present state of a living tradition and remain open to revision. They are a way of seeing, notated — to be carried lightly, used, and, where they prove unsound, reworked.</i></p>

The Master Principle of Amovera

All that persists does so by acquiring what it lacks.

Amovera is a philosophical system developed across roughly fifteen years of dialectical work. Its Master Principle — derived rather than asserted — serves as a spine from which every framework hangs. The eight frameworks below are best understood as eight aspects of a single principle, addressed to different domains of human life.

This page is a directory and an invitation. The full body of work lives at amovera.net. A dialectical web tool called Parrhesia is forthcoming — opening to the public once its underlying economy is in place.

The Eight Frameworks

  • Structural Absurdism
    A reading of the human condition that takes absurdity not as a wound but as a structural feature to be worked with.
  • The Compass of Agape
    An orientation for love that does not collapse into sentiment, hierarchy, or coercion.
  • Transcendental Persona Modification
    A discipline for the deliberate refinement of the self one presents to the world.
  • Equitalism · Personal Warranty Company
    An economic framework grounded in personal warrant rather than abstract capital.
  • Autonomous Nations Theory
    A political framework for sovereignty that does not require the existing nation-state form.
  • S.T.A.M.P.
    An investigative and recruitment instrument carried in pamphlets and zines.
  • The Court of Amovera
    A deliberative form by which questions of warrant and conduct can be adjudicated within the system.
  • The Sovereign Blueprint
    A synthesis of technological, mental, economic, and political sovereignty into a unified instrument.

Companion Works

The system is accompanied by several practical instruments: the Amovera Deck, a 64-card contemplative system pairing Gospel of Thomas logia with Genesis passages across four alchemical suits; Parrhesia, a dialectical web application (forthcoming); and the Amovera Suite, a set of software projects including the time-management application Optima and the agent-based simulation Project Anix.
